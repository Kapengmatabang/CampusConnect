About Us

Built for PLMUN, by PLMUN

CampusConnect is the official student organization recruitment and campus engagement platform of Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Muntinlupa.

What We Do

A single home for student org life

Before CampusConnect, joining a student organization meant asking around, printing forms, and hoping your application didn't get lost. CampusConnect brings that entire process online, students can browse every accredited organization, apply in minutes, and track their status in real time.

Organizations get a simpler way to reach applicants, run interviews, and manage members, while the university keeps a clear, centralized record of student involvement across every college.

Why We Exist

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide an accessible and user-friendly digital platform that connects PLMUN students with campus organizations while promoting participation, leadership, collaboration, and personal growth.

Our Vision

To become the official digital gateway for student organizations at PLMUN, creating an inclusive and active campus community where every student has the opportunity to engage, contribute, and succeed.
