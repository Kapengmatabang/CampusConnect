What You Can Do in CampusConnect?

Discover Student Organizations

Browse accredited student clubs and organizations based on your interests, academic programs, and extracurricular activities.

Apply Online

Submit organization applications without the need for printed forms. Track your application status in real time.

Stay Updated

Receive announcements about recruitment periods, campus activities, seminars, workshops, competitions, and university events.

See upcoming organization activities through an organized event calendar.

Get Notifications

Receive notifications regarding announcements, application updates, interview schedules, and event reminders.

Interact with organizations and become part of a vibrant student community that encourages collaboration and leadership.
